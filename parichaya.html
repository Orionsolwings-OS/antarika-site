Antarika Parichaya

✦

Antarika Parichaya is an introduction to exploring your inner world through observation, reflection, and meditation. The programme offers a guided beginning for those who wish to understand Dhyaan and establish a regular Sadhana practice.

Whether you are new to meditation or wish to strengthen your existing practice, Antarika Parichaya provides an opportunity to learn, observe, and reflect under the guidance of a Margadarshi.

What You Will Explore

1

Chitta Avalokana

Observation of the Mind

Learn to notice thoughts, emotions, distractions, and reactions without immediately judging them.

2

Atma Pariseelana

Self-Reflection

Develop the habit of observing your thoughts, intentions, and behaviour to cultivate self-awareness.

3

Ekagrata Abhyasa

Practice of Concentration

Learn to gently bring your attention back to the meditation method prescribed by the Margadarshi.

Your 5-Day Experience

Participate in guided sessions, practise meditation, and reflect on your daily experiences. Each session introduces an opportunity to develop awareness and build a foundation for continued Sadhana. The programme focuses on learning and consistent practice. It does not promise instant spiritual attainment or Siddhi.

Who Can Join?

Antarika Parichaya is an introductory programme for people who wish to explore meditation, understand their inner experiences, and begin or strengthen their Dhyaan and Sadhana practice. Both beginners and those with previous meditation experience are welcome.

Our Approach

At Antarika, the journey begins with observation, deepens through self-reflection, and progresses through sustained practice. We encourage participants to approach Sadhana with patience, discipline, sincerity, and guidance from a qualified Margadarshi.
